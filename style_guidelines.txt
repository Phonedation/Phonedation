Overall Look and Feel
===========================
The overall look and feel should be minimalist, flat and elegant. We avoid 3D buttons, gradients, rounded "bubbly" shapes and bright colours.
The site should feel calm and trustworthy, with lots of white space.
Every page uses the same header, footer, black call-to-action box and chatbot so the site feels consistent.

Colours
===========================
For PhoneBuilder, colours show that we are simple, honest and premium. We use an almost
black-and-white palette so the phone and the prices stand out.
These are our colour swatches:

- #0B0B0B  Black: main text, headings, black buttons, black boxes, the "popular" pricing card
- #FFFFFF  White: page background, text on black
- #F6F5F2  Cream: background behind the phone picture and the chatbot's messages
- #6F6D69  Grey: softer text (intro paragraphs, small titles, menu links, footer)
- #E4E2DE  Line: thin borders and dividing lines

Muted green and muted red are only used inside the builder to show budget effects
("Saves $70" / "Puts you $94 over budget").

Iconography
===========================
We keep icons to a minimum and use only flat, thin-line icons that match the overall look and feel.
The phone on the home page is drawn with CSS.
When selecting an icon, please select the correct icon for the job and keep the following in mind:

- Use simple text symbols where they work: → on "Start your build" buttons, + to open FAQ items, × to close the chat
- Icons should be outlined, 1px lines, in black or grey only
- No filled, coloured, emoji or 3D icons
- Circles are used for team member initials and colour swatches, keep them thin-outlined

Typography
===========================
We use two fonts from Google Fonts:

- Cormorant Garamond (italic, 400/500) for headings, the logo, prices, step numbers, quotes and FAQ questions
- Jost (300/400) for body text, menu links, buttons and labels

Sizes:
- h1: 80px (52px on phones), h2: 48px (36px on phones), h3: 26px
- Body text: 17px, light weight (300), line height 1.7
- Small titles above headings: 11px, UPPERCASE, spaced out (letter-spacing 4px), grey
- Intro paragraphs: 20px, grey

Links
===========================
Links will not be underlined. Menu links are grey, uppercase and spaced out (12px, letter-spacing 3px).
When a user hovers over a menu link, it turns black and gets a thin 1px line under it.
The link for the page you are on stays black with the line under it.

Buttons
===========================
Buttons will be flat, with square corners, a 1px black border and no shadow.
Button text is uppercase, 12px, with wide letter spacing (3px).

- Main button: black background, white text
- Secondary button: white background, black text
- On hover, the colours swap (black becomes white and white becomes black)
- On black backgrounds, buttons are white with a white outline on hover so they stay visible
- The chatbot's quick-question buttons are smaller (13px, normal case) but keep the same flat, outlined style
