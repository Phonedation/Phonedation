FAQ

Questions

Does it cost anything to design a phone?

No. The assistant is free to use. You only pay if you order the phone.

Why is there a $199 base price?

Every phone needs a frame, a 5G modem, speakers and sensors, and has to be assembled and tested. That cost is the same for everyone.

Can I change parts in a build?

Yes. Every build is just a starting point. Swap any part and the price updates.
